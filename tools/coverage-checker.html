The three questions behind the tool

Who pays for physical therapy after a car accident in Michigan?

Your own auto insurer, through no-fault PIP benefits — regardless of fault, with no preset visit limit for reasonably necessary accident care — out-of-pocket cost depends on the policy's elected PIP medical level and deductible.

Does insurance limit how many physical therapy visits I can have?

Commercial plans commonly allow 20–60 visits per benefit year; Medicare has no hard cap (a documentation threshold applies); Michigan auto no-fault and workers' comp set no preset visit counts — medical necessity (and, for auto, the policy's PIP level) governs.

Can I start physical therapy without a referral in Michigan?

Yes — Michigan direct access allows evaluation and treatment without a prescription for up to 10 visits or 21 days (wellness services exempt). Some insurance plans still require a referral for payment, which the clinic verifies and obtains.
