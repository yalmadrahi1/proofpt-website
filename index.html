Answers to the questions we hear most

When does Proof Physical Therapy open?

Proof opens February 2027 at 44056 Mound Rd, Suite 103, Sterling Heights, MI. You can request an appointment now and a real person follows up within one business day — it isn't a booked appointment yet.

Do I need a referral to start physical therapy in Michigan?

Usually not to start. Michigan direct access allows up to 10 visits or 21 days of treatment without a physician referral; some insurance plans still require one for payment, so we verify your coverage first. More in our referral guide.

What will it cost, and do you take my insurance?

Cost depends on your plan and the care you need. Proof is in-network with Medicare, BCBSM/BCN, Priority Health, HAP, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, and Michigan Medicaid; Michigan no-fault and workers' comp follow their own rules. We verify your specific benefits before your first visit — see insurance & coverage.

Do you have more than one location?

No — Proof is one independent clinic in Sterling Heights, serving nearby Metro Detroit communities. We don't operate other branches; areas we serve all travel to the one clinic.
