Injured in a car accident?

Michigan no-fault benefits cover physical therapy for accident injuries, regardless of fault — no preset visit limit, and many patients owe nothing out of pocket, depending on their policy's PIP level. We verify your claim before the first appointment and document your recovery with objective testing.

Insurance & what therapy costs

Straight answer: we're credentialing now with Medicare, Medicare Advantage, BCBSM/BCN, Priority Health, HAP, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, and Michigan Medicaid plans — participation is confirmed and published before opening. Michigan auto no-fault and workers' compensation claims don't require network contracts, and every patient's benefits are verified before the first visit.

Before your first visit

We verify your exact benefits — deductible, visit limits, referral requirements — and tell you what to expect in writing. No surprise billing.

Auto accident (no-fault)

Reasonably necessary accident care is covered with no preset visit limit; your share depends on your policy's PIP level. We verify the claim first.
