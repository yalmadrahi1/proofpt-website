How we hold ourselves to "measured care"

  1. Baseline on day one. A full evaluation with objective range-of-motion, strength, and validated function scores — the day-one numbers everything is measured against.
  2. A goal tied to your life. Your plan targets a specific activity you want back — a job demand, a sport, a stair — not a generic "feel better."
  3. Re-tested at every milestone. We re-measure on a schedule and put the change in writing, so progress is a number you can see, not a feeling.
  4. Discharge is a clinical decision, not a visit count. We plan toward your goals and the measures that track them, and decide together when you’re ready to carry them forward safely — or explain plainly why the plan is changing.
