How we treat post-surgical rehab

Joint replacements, rotator cuff repairs, ACL reconstruction, spinal surgery — every protocol is different, and we follow your surgeon's to the letter while progressing you as fast as tissue healing allows. Progress reports go back to your surgeon at every milestone without being requested, and your full plan of care is scheduled up front so recovery never waits.

Signs this page is about you

  • Scheduled for surgery and want to 'prehab'
  • Post-op and unsure if progress is on track
  • Stiffness or weakness months after a procedure
  • Cleared for therapy and choosing a clinic
Michigan direct access: most patients can start physical therapy without a physician referral (10 visits / 21 days before a prescription is needed; wellness services exempt). Insurance plan rules vary — we verify yours and obtain any required referral before visit one.

Common questions

When should I schedule physical therapy for a planned surgery?

Before the surgery. Pre-operative strengthening ('prehab') measurably improves post-op outcomes, and booking your post-op slots in advance means zero gap after clearance.

What if I'm behind the 'normal' recovery timeline?

Timelines are averages. We measure where you actually are — motion, strength, function — and adjust the plan. Being behind is a reason for better dosing, not discouragement.

Does Medicare cover post-surgical physical therapy?

Yes. Medicare Part B covers medically necessary outpatient therapy with no hard visit cap — documentation requirements apply past an annual threshold, which is our job to handle, not yours.
