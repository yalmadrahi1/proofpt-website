How we treat foot & ankle pain

Feet take 4,000–10,000 loaded steps a day, so small mechanical problems become big painful ones. We treat plantar fasciitis with progressive loading (the approach with the strongest evidence), rebuild chronically unstable ankles with balance and strength work, and manage Achilles problems with the graded tendon-loading programs that actually resolve them.

Signs this page is about you

  • First-steps-in-the-morning heel pain
  • Ankles that roll or feel untrustworthy
  • Achilles stiffness and pain with activity
  • Arch fatigue or pain after standing shifts
Michigan direct access: most patients can start physical therapy without a physician referral (10 visits / 21 days before a prescription is needed; wellness services exempt). Insurance plan rules vary — we verify yours and obtain any required referral before visit one.

Common questions

How long does plantar fasciitis take to resolve?

With a proper loading program, most patients improve meaningfully in 6–8 weeks, with full resolution over 3–4 months. Cortisone and rest alone commonly relapse — loading changes the tissue.

Do I need custom orthotics?

Sometimes. We test whether support actually changes your symptoms before recommending them — and we fit them on-site when they earn their place in the plan.

Why does my ankle keep re-spraining?

Because sprains heal ligaments but not the balance-and-reaction system that protects them. Rebuilding that system is exactly what rehab is for — and it's why re-sprain rates drop sharply after a proper program.
